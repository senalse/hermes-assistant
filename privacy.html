Privacy Policy — Hermes Assistant

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who uses this app

Hermes Assistant is a self-hosted personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not available to anyone else.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's own Gmail messages and Google Calendar events through Google's APIs, in order to read, summarise, draft and (only after the owner's explicit approval) send email or change calendar events.

How data is stored and shared

  • Google OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Email and calendar content is processed on the owner's computer and sent to an AI model provider only as needed to answer the owner's requests.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than assisting the owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Contact the app owner using the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
